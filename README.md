# Ant Foraging Analysis
Explored the complex distributed system of a ants' nest. In this case scenario, starting from the original version created by Uri Wilensky, I added some upgrades to work with enviromental problems in ant foraging. 
In particular, I wanted to simulate the behavior of pluvial ants that live on trees. 

## Pluvial ants 
Pluvial ants studies demonstrated that those kind of ants live to change paths everyday. Their paths aren't build, they're found everyday because their environment changes constantly. They live on trees, and use branches to move when looking for food, but their roads can break, can change due to seasons, etc... 

## My simulation
Sort of a labyrinth, with more than one possibile road to reach food source. Exactly how the pluvial ants move, with more than one possibility, the experiment was to see which path they will follow **MOSTLY**.
